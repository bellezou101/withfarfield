Product

From a fix nobody sees to a fix that holds.

Farfield captures every reprompt and edit as employees work, groups them into patterns, and turns each pattern into a fix you can verify. Employees don’t do anything extra, and data is masked by default.

Book a demo
One workspace

Everyone looks at the same evidence.

Operations, data, and engineering review the same recurring patterns, the same instances, and the same before-and-after.

1 · Capture

Nothing extra for employees to do.

Captures every reprompt in AI chat tools like Claude and ChatGPT, then everything downstream in Mail, Word, Excel and PowerPoint, each linked to the workflow it belongs to. Data is masked by default.

2 · Find patterns

See what keeps repeating.

Groups the same correction across employees and teams, and measures the rework time each one causes.

Leaders see high-level patterns by change type and team, week over week.

3 · Recommend

Turn a pattern into a fix someone can approve.

Each recurring correction becomes a proposed fix: an instruction change a team lead can approve, or an engineering ticket with the real examples attached.

4 · Verify

A fix that works stays fixed.

Every fix is backtested against past corrections before it ships. A change that would bring an old error back fails the release check.

5 · Track results

Know what each fix changed.

After a fix goes live, Farfield tracks how often drafts needed it before and after, and when the target was reached.

Who uses it

Built with different views in mind.

Analysts

Their own edits: where they lose the most time and which edits they keep making. It doubles as coaching.

Leaders

High-level patterns: the types of changes being made and which teams are affected, week over week.

Engineers

Exact instances: the real examples behind each pattern, and a prioritized fix list for each team, ranked by urgency and rework hours.
