One workspace

Everyone looks at the same evidence.

Operations, data, and engineering review the same recurring patterns, the same instances, and the same before-and-after.

1 · Capture

Nothing extra for employees to do.

Re-prompts and edits are captured as people work in AI chat tools, Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and email, each linked to the workflow it belongs to.

2 · Find patterns

See what keeps repeating.

Corrections are clustered across employees and teams into recurring failure patterns, ranked by frequency and the time each one costs.

3 · Reveal what AI is missing

Turn a pattern into a fix someone can approve.

Each pattern becomes a proposed fix: an instruction change a team lead can approve, or an engineering ticket with the evidence attached.

4 · Test before it ships

A fix that works stays fixed.

Every fix is backtested against historical corrections. Changes that would reintroduce a corrected error fail the release check.

5 · Watch whether rework falls

Know what each fix changed.

After a fix goes live, Farfield tracks how often drafts needed it before and after, and when the target was reached.
